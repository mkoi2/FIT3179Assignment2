Effects Of Greenhouse Gases In The Atmosphere

Greenhouse gases are gases that trap heat in the atmosphere and are an important factor for Earth to sustain life, however with human's industrial activities the amount of gases being released into the atmosphere is too great leading to global warming and climate change. The main gases that make up greenhouse gases include carbon dioxide, methane and nitrous oxide. The contribution to global greenhouse gas emissions of each of these gases can be observed in the waffle chart.

Carbon dioxide is the most prominent gas emitted. It is produced when fossil fuels are burned, industrial production and land use changes.

Methane is produced within agriculture, fossil fuel production and waste decomposition in landfills.

Nitrous oxide is primarily produced from agriculture, when nitrogen fertilizers treat the soils.
