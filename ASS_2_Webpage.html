Exploring Enviromental Sustainablility Indicators Around The World

Environmental sustainability indicators are ways to measure human impact on the Earth. It is important to uphold sustainable and environmentally friendly practices to maintain a safe and liveable

Greenhouse Gas Emissions

Greenhouse gases are gases that trap heat in the atmosphere and are an important factor for Earth to sustain life, however with human activity the amount of gases being released into the atmosphere is too great leading to global warming and climate change.
